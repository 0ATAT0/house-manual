Front door

To lock it

Lift the handle, let it back to neutral, then turn the key

Lifting the handle is the first locking mechanism: it throws the bolts. Let the handle come back to neutral, then turn the key to lock it off. The door feels shut either way, so it's worth checking.

Feeding

Four meals a day each, plus dry left down overnight. The amount changes by meal.

Breakfast 7:00 – 7:30am 80gWet, 1 tin
Lunch 12:30 – 1:00pm 20gDry
First dinner 5:30 – 6:00pm 40gWet
Second dinner 9:00 – 9:30pm 40gWet
Overnight Left down at bedtime ~20gDry

Serving the wet food

It needs to be in chunks, more so for Luna. Pressing a fork into it does the job. A bit of water over the top helps with hydration.

If you're out all day

Leave the dry lunch down when you go in the morning, 20g each, and they'll eat it when they want it. Breakfast and both dinners stay as they are.

If one of them isn't eating

Luna eats much less than Fred and sometimes needs it held out on a fork to get her going. Normal for her.

Fred skipping a meal is unusual. If he skips two in a row, drop us a message.

Good to know

Saying hello

Staring at them wide-eyed freaks them out after a while. Slow blinks are how you say all good. Neither is a lap cat at the moment, and both prefer scratches with their feet on the ground to being held for them.

Sleep

They sleep a lot through the day, roughly 10am to 5pm, waking for lunch. Their beds are in our room, but they're rarely there all night.

Play

The vet says fifteen minutes a day is fine for a solo cat, and these two play with each other well past that, so nothing is needed from you. Mornings and evenings are when they play most. If they're still going when you want to sleep, shut the bedroom door.

Litter tray

Usually morning and evening. Luna is better at covering it, Fred is not. Scoop it when you notice.

Poo goes down the toilet, with as much litter shaken off it as you can manage. Pee clumps go in the bin.

It stinks, and there's no way around it. Every scent-masking option is toxic to animals except candles. Open a window with the cats shut in another room.

Brush, harnesses and leads

Tall cupboard under the stairs. The harnesses and leads are in the tote hanging on the door.

If Fred gets a mucky bum

Rare, but he sometimes has a runny poo that catches his fur. He's had a sanitary trim to minimise it. If it happens anyway, wet toilet paper is the easiest fix and trimming it out is the best one: he's docile enough to hold in one arm and trim with the other.

Luna is a nightmare for trims, but she's better on the loo, so it shouldn't come up.
