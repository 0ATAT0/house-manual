Break the wet food up

It wants to be in chunks rather than a block, Luna especially. Pressing a fork into it does the job. A splash of water over the top helps with hydration.

They eat at different speeds

Fred is a bit of a pig and will finish first. Luna eats much less and sometimes needs encouraging: hold a bit out on the fork to get her going.

Getting on with them

Slow blinks, not stares

Staring at them wide-eyed freaks them out after a while. A slow blink is how you tell them everything's fine.

Laps

Neither of them is much of a lap cat at the moment, so don't take it personally.

Sleep

They sleep most of the day. They'll surface for lunch, but roughly 10am to 5pm they're out. Their beds are in our room, though they're rarely in them all night.

Play

Having two means they entertain each other. The vet's line is fifteen minutes a day for a solo cat, and they well exceed that between themselves, so there's nothing you need to do. Mornings and evenings are their liveliest. If they're still going when you want to sleep, just shut the bedroom door.

Litter tray

They usually go in the morning and again in the evening. It stinks, and there's no way around it: every scent-masking product is toxic to animals apart from candles. Scoop it when you notice it, and open a window if the cats are shut safely away.
