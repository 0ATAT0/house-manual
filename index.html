Serving the wet food

It wants to be in chunks rather than a block, Luna especially. Pressing a fork into it does the job. A splash of water over the top helps with hydration.

If one of them isn't eating

Luna eats much less than Fred and often needs a fork held out to get her going. That's normal for her.

Fred turning down a meal is the odd one. If he skips two in a row, call us.

Living with them

Saying hello

Staring at them wide-eyed freaks them out after a while. A slow blink is how you tell them everything's fine. Neither is much of a lap cat at the moment, so don't take it personally.

Sleep

Out cold from roughly 10am to 5pm, surfacing for lunch. Their beds are in our room, though they're rarely in them all night.

Play

The vet's line is fifteen minutes a day for a solo cat, and these two well exceed that between themselves, so there's nothing you need to do. Mornings and evenings are their liveliest. If they're still going when you want to sleep, shut the bedroom door.

Litter tray

Morning and evening, usually. Luna buries hers properly, Fred makes no effort at all. Scoop it when you notice.
